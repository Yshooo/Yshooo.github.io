      How the score works
      
    
        
  • Rules (25): no em dash, en dash, double hyphen, spaced hyphen or semicolon. Each one costs 8.
    • 
        
  • Wording (25): flags words and phrases that show up far more in AI writing than in people's writing (leverage, passionate, delve, spearheaded, "I am confident", and many more). Each one costs 5.
    • 
        
  • Specific to you (25): counts numbers, names and tools from your experience that appear in the answer. Numbers and names that are not in your experience, the question or the job post get flagged as possibly invented, and each costs 5.
    • 
        
  • Human rhythm (15): people vary sentence length a lot. AI tends to write sentences of similar length, starts many with "I", stacks lists of three, and uses "not only... but also" style turns.
    • 
        
  • Fit (10): stays within your word limit and actually uses the words of the question.
    • 
      

      
It is a heuristic, not an AI detector. A high score means the draft avoids the usual tells and sticks to your facts. Always read it out loud before submitting.
